# Advanced 2024–2026 ICT Concepts: Opening Gaps (NWOG/NDOG), Inversion FVGs & Universal Multi-Asset Delivery

## Summary of Modern Framework (2024–2026)
Across his 2024, 2025, and 2026 teachings and market reviews, Michael J. Huddleston ("ICT") expanded his core methodology to emphasize how central bank algorithms utilize **Opening Gaps** and **Imbalance Inversions** across all financial markets (Forex, Commodities, and Index Futures). 

Rather than relying on indicators, the algorithm delivers price through a hierarchy of **Time and Price** arrays:
1. **New Week Opening Gap (NWOG):** Gap between Friday 17:00 NY close and Sunday 18:00 NY open.
2. **New Day Opening Gap (NDOG):** Gap between 16:59:59 NY close and 18:00 NY open.
3. **9:30 AM Opening Range Gap (ORG):** Gap created at the 09:30 NY cash market open.
4. **Inversion Fair Value Gaps (IFVG):** Fair Value Gaps that price trades completely through and fails, causing them to flip polarity from support to resistance (or vice-versa).
5. **Balanced Price Range (BPR):** Overlapping bullish and bearish imbalances that price neutralizes before making an explosive directional run.

## Concrete Trading Rules

- **RULE:** The New Week Opening Gap (NWOG) and New Day Opening Gap (NDOG) must be plotted horizontally across the charts as dynamic support, resistance, and liquidity magnets.
  - **CATEGORY:** reference level
  - **NUMBERS:** NWOG: Friday 17:00 NY to Sunday 18:00 NY; NDOG: 17:00 NY to 18:00 NY daily reset.
  - **SOURCE:** 2024 Mentorship & NDOG Series (Said & Showed).
  - **CLARITY:** Clear.

- **RULE:** The 50% midpoint (Consequent Encroachment) of any NWOG or NDOG represents the primary institutional balance point; price often oscillates around or repels sharply from this level.
  - **CATEGORY:** reference level
  - **NUMBERS:** 50.0% (Consequent Encroachment).
  - **SOURCE:** 2024 Mentorship (Said & Showed).
  - **CLARITY:** Clear.

- **RULE:** An Inversion Fair Value Gap (IFVG) occurs when a previously established Fair Value Gap is completely violated and closed through with displacement; the breached FVG then acts as opposing support/resistance on the subsequent re-test.
  - **CATEGORY:** entry / confirmation
  - **NUMBERS:** 1-minute to 5-minute timeframes.
  - **SOURCE:** 2023/2024 Mentorship & Inversion Lectures (Said & Showed).
  - **CLARITY:** Clear.

- **RULE:** A Balanced Price Range (BPR) is created when an aggressive impulse move leaves a Fair Value Gap that is immediately retraced and overlapped by an opposing Fair Value Gap; retests into this overlapping pocket provide high-probability continuation entries.
  - **CATEGORY:** entry
  - **NUMBERS:** Overlapping zone of opposing FVGs.
  - **SOURCE:** 2023/2024 Mentorship (Said & Showed).
  - **CLARITY:** Clear.

- **RULE:** The delivery algorithm is universal across asset classes (Forex, Commodities, Indices, Cryptos). Execution parameters adjust to the asset's specific tick/pip size, spread, and active volume sessions, but the core Time and Price mechanics remain identical.
  - **CATEGORY:** general principle
  - **NUMBERS:** Universal application.
  - **SOURCE:** 2022–2026 Mentorships (Reiterated continuously).
  - **CLARITY:** Clear.

- **RULE:** Order execution must adapt to price speed: use limit orders at the FVG boundary/CE when price is retracing cleanly; use real-time market execution when price forms a micro 3-candle confirmation swing or rapidly accelerates away after tapping an imbalance.
  - **CATEGORY:** entry / execution
  - **NUMBERS:** Real-time adaptive order routing.
  - **SOURCE:** Tape Reading & Execution Drills (Said & Showed).
  - **CLARITY:** Clear.
