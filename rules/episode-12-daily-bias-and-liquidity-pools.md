## Summary of Episode
In this episode, Michael J. Huddleston (ICT) introduces advanced price action theory regarding market structure, contrasting his refined algorithm-based approach with traditional retail concepts (such as simple higher-high/lower-low definitions or Larry Williams' classic structure). 

The core of the lesson focuses on classifying swing highs and swing lows into three distinct tiers: **Short-Term**, **Intermediate-Term**, and **Long-Term**. Crucially, ICT teaches that intermediate-term swing points are not merely defined by adjacent short-term swings, but are also fundamentally generated when price rebalances an existing imbalance (Fair Value Gap). He demonstrates how to use this structural hierarchy to validate order blocks, define low-risk vs. aggressive entry points, determine when a trade bias has been invalidated, and project precise downside targets using Fibonacci extensions anchored from intermediate-term highs.

## Concrete Trading Rules

### 1. Traditional Market Structure Classification
* **RULE:** Define a **Short-Term High (STH)** as any candlestick high flanked by a lower high on its immediate left and right; define a **Short-Term Low (STL)** as any candlestick low flanked by a higher low on its immediate left and right.
* **CATEGORY:** Reference level
* **NUMBERS:** 3-bar swing pattern (timeframe-independent).
* **SOURCE:** [05:27] - [06:05] (Showed and explained on 1-Hour chart).
* **CLARITY:** Clear.

### 2. Intermediate-Term Swing Point Definition (Traditional vs. Algorithmic)
* **RULE:** Classify a swing high as an **Intermediate-Term High (ITH)** if it is flanked by a lower STH on its left and right, *OR* if it is a swing high that forms immediately upon the rebalancing of an old bearish Fair Value Gap (imbalance). Classify a swing low as an **Intermediate-Term Low (ITL)** if it is flanked by a higher STL on its left and right, *OR* if it is a swing low that forms immediately upon the rebalancing of an old bullish Fair Value Gap.
* **CATEGORY:** Reference level
* **NUMBERS:** Not stated (applies to any timeframe).
* **SOURCE:** [05:50] - [06:05], [13:17] - [14:15] (Said and showed on chart).
* **CLARITY:** Clear, but requires algorithmic logic to track whether a swing point coincides with the filling of a Fair Value Gap.

### 3. Long-Term Swing Point Definition
* **RULE:** Classify a swing high as a **Long-Term High (LTH)** if it is flanked by a lower Intermediate-Term High (ITH) on its left and right, *OR* if it forms directly at a higher-timeframe reference level (such as a Daily FVG boundary). Classify a swing low as a **Long-Term Low (LTL)** if it is flanked by a higher Intermediate-Term Low (ITL) on its left and right, *OR* if it forms directly at a higher-timeframe reference level.
* **CATEGORY:** Reference level
* **NUMBERS:** Not stated.
* **SOURCE:** [05:43] - [05:52], [11:15] - [12:15] (Said and showed on chart).
* **CLARITY:** Clear.

### 4. Market Weakness / Failed Swing Rule
* **RULE:** If an Intermediate-Term High (ITH) forms and fails to exceed adjacent swing highs or a preceding LTH, it indicates extreme market weakness, confirming a bearish trend projection.
* **CATEGORY:** Confirmation
* **NUMBERS:** Not stated.
* **SOURCE:** [14:35] - [15:15], [17:50] - [18:18] (Said and showed on chart).
* **CLARITY:** Clear.

### 5. Bearish Institutional Order Flow Rule
* **RULE:** In a bearish market structure, up-close candles (or consecutive up-close candles prior to a down-move) act as bearish order blocks (resistance) and must not be closed above by price.
* **CATEGORY:** Confirmation
* **NUMBERS:** Not stated.
* **SOURCE:** [18:40] - [19:15], [21:55] - [22:30] (Said and showed on chart).
* **CLARITY:** Clear.

### 6. Bullish Institutional Order Flow Rule
* **RULE:** In a bullish market structure, down-close candles (or consecutive down-close candles prior to an up-move) act as bullish order blocks (support) and must not be closed below by price.
* **CATEGORY:** Confirmation
* **NUMBERS:** Not stated.
* **SOURCE:** [22:20] - [22:40] (Said).
* **CLARITY:** Clear.

### 7. Aggressive Lower-Timeframe Entry Rule
* **RULE:** When higher-timeframe analysis is bearish and price reaches a bearish order block or FVG, execute an aggressive short entry on a lower timeframe (e.g., 5-minute or 15-minute) immediately upon the formation of a lower-timeframe FVG on a candlestick breakdown, without waiting for a break of a short-term or intermediate-term low.
* **CATEGORY:** Entry
* **NUMBERS:** 15-minute, 5-minute, or 1-minute execution timeframes.
* **SOURCE:** [16:45] - [17:15], [22:24] - [23:10] (Said and showed on chart).
* **CLARITY:** Clear.

### 8. Low-Risk / High-Confirmation Entry Rule
* **RULE:** Execute a low-risk, high-confirmation short entry only after price breaks a lower-timeframe Intermediate-Term Low (ITL) or Short-Term Low (STL) (signaling a Market Structure Shift), and then retraces back up into the newly formed bearish FVG or Order Block.
* **CATEGORY:** Entry
* **NUMBERS:** 15-minute, 5-minute, or 1-minute execution timeframes.
* **SOURCE:** [17:10] - [17:35], [24:50] - [25:55] (Said and showed on chart).
* **CLARITY:** Clear.

### 9. Fibonacci Projection Target Rule
* **RULE:** To project bearish downside targets, anchor the Fibonacci tool from the Intermediate-Term High (ITH) representing the retracement high that initiated the sell-off (not the absolute highest LTH of the entire move) down to the subsequent significant swing low (or Long-Term Low). The primary downside target is the -1.5 Standard Deviation.
* **CATEGORY:** Take profit
* **NUMBERS:** -1.5 Standard Deviation.
* **SOURCE:** [20:00] - [21:40], [23:50] - [25:58] (Said and showed on chart).
* **CLARITY:** Clear.

* **Target / Take Profit:** projected using Fibonacci from the ITH down to the LTL. The -1.5 extension aligned exactly with the daily sell-side liquidity target [24:50 - 25:58].
* **Outcome:** Price collapsed rapidly, hitting the -1.5 target precisely.

## Conditions that Cancel a Setup / Filters (What NOT to do)

1. **Violation of LTH/LTL or ITH/ITL:** If a trade is entered short based on a bearish bias, and price trades cleanly above the defined Intermediate-Term High (ITH) or Long-Term High (LTH), the trade setup is immediately invalidated. The trader must exit/cancel all remaining positions.
2. **"Coloring Outside the Lines" Filter:** If a market is highly volatile and trades significantly beyond the boundaries of an order block (more than a minor wick fluctuation), the validity of that block is compromised.
3. **Avoid Myopic Structure Analysis:** Do not define market structure simply by basic, consecutive higher-highs and higher-lows. This retail perspective results in false trend-change signals.
4. **No-Trade Filter during Extreme Uncertainty:** If there is high geopolitical/global uncertainty causing chaotic price action with a lack of clear draw on liquidity, go neutral and do not trade.

## Ambiguities and Open Questions

1. **Quantinement of "Coloring Outside the Lines":** ICT mentions that the algorithm sometimes allows price to "color outside the lines" (trade slightly past the order block or FVG boundaries) during volatile conditions. He notes that handling this is a matter of "experience," leaving the exact threshold for algorithmic invalidation (e.g., maximum pip/point overshoot or close-percentage above/below the block) subjective and open to trader interpretation.
2. **Rebalance Timing Threshold:** The rule that an Intermediate-Term High/Low is formed "immediately upon the rebalancing of an old imbalance" does not define a strict limit on the number of candlesticks or the percentage of FVG fill required to qualify the swing. This must be strictly defined by the programmer (e.g., within $X$ bars of a $Y\%$ FVG fill).
