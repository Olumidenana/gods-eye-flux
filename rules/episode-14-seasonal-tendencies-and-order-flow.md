# Episode 14: 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 14

**Source URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NUdu1n-ML98

## Summary of Episode
This episode is a concise, live-narrated execution demonstration on the Micro E-mini Nasdaq-100 Index Futures (MNQ) 30-second chart. Michael (ICT) demonstrates how to identify an institutional entry setup using a lower-timeframe Market Structure Shift (MSS) and a return to a bullish Fair Value Gap (FVG). He showcases how to manage the trade using limit orders, target a daily gap-fill level, monitor S&P 500 futures for correlated confirmation, and handle scaling out versus full-target exits.

## Concrete Trading Rules

*   **RULE:** A long trade setup is validated on a lower-timeframe chart when a Market Structure Shift (MSS) to the upside occurs, leaving behind a bullish Fair Value Gap (FVG) to which price subsequently retraces.
    *   **CATEGORY:** Entry
    *   **NUMBERS:** 30-second timeframe.
    *   **SOURCE:** 0:03 - 0:24 (Showed on chart / Said).
    *   **CLARITY:** Clear.

*   **RULE:** Gaps created between Friday's close and Sunday's open serve as highly probable high-timeframe draws on liquidity (gap-fill targets).
    *   **CATEGORY:** Reference level
    *   **NUMBERS:** "Friday's close".
    *   **SOURCE:** 0:11 (Said).
    *   **CLARITY:** Clear.

*   **RULE:** During trade management, the trader must focus exclusively on the candle behavior and price progression toward reference levels rather than monitoring the floating monetary profit/loss (PnL) display.
    *   **CATEGORY:** General principle
    *   **NUMBERS:** Not stated.
    *   **SOURCE:** 1:10 (Said).
    *   **CLARITY:** Clear.

*   **RULE:** A short-term high formed prior to 10:00 AM New York time acts as a buy-side liquidity pool that can be used for taking partial profits and trailing stops.
    *   **CATEGORY:** Reference level
    *   **NUMBERS:** 10:00 AM NY time.
    *   **SOURCE:** 2:03 (Showed on chart / Said).
    *   **CLARITY:** Clear.

*   **RULE:** When price reaches a primary short-term buy-side liquidity target, scale out 50% of the position and trail the stop loss to breakeven ("even").
    *   **CATEGORY:** Trade management
    *   **NUMBERS:** 50% (e.g., 6 contracts of a 12-contract position).
    *   **SOURCE:** 2:56 - 3:08 (Showed on chart / Said).
    *   **CLARITY:** Clear.

*   **RULE:** Monitor the Micro E-mini S&P 500 index futures (MES) alongside the Nasdaq (MNQ) on identical lower timeframes; if both indices are concurrently expanding towards their respective short-term highs, it serves as correlated confluence to hold the trade.
    *   **CATEGORY:** Confirmation
    *   **NUMBERS:** Not stated.
    *   **SOURCE:** 3:26 - 3:38 (Showed on chart / Said).
    *   **CLARITY:** Clear.

## Chart Examples

### Chart Example 1
*   **Timestamp:** 0:01 - 4:28
*   **Instrument:** Micro Nasdaq-100 Index Futures (MNQH2022)
*   **Timeframe:** 30-second
*   **Date/Time (if visible):** Feb 28, 2022 (approx. 09:40 to 10:20 AM NY Time)
*   **Reference Levels identified:** 
    *   Bullish Fair Value Gap at 14072.00.
    *   Short-term buy-side liquidity pool above 14160.00 (high formed prior to 10:00 AM).
    *   Friday's close / Gap fill target area (up to 14220.00).
*   **Liquidity Sweep / Price Action:** A market structure shift to the upside occurred, followed by a retracement back down into the 30s bullish FVG.
*   **Entry point / trigger:** Buy Limit order executed at 14072.00 (exactly at the low of the pullback candle inside the FVG).
*   **Stop Loss placement:** 14041.50 (placed below the swing low of the market structure shift setup).
*   **Target / Take Profit:** Originally planned to take 50% (6 contracts) partials at 14180.00 and leave the rest for 14220.00. Later adjusted to a full flat exit of all 12 contracts at 14220.00.
*   **Outcome:** Trade successfully reached the limit order target at 14220.00 for a full profit realization.
