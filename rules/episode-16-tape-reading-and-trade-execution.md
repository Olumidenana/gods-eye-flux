## Summary of Episode
Episode 16 focuses on trading multiple setups within both the morning (AM) and afternoon (PM) sessions in New York Index Futures (specifically the E-mini S&P 500 Futures). ICT explains the top-down narrative: anchoring daily institutional bias via daily order blocks and relative equal highs/lows, applying a swing projection model using Fibonacci anchored to candle bodies (not wicks) to project expansion targets, identifying the Judas swing relative to opening prices (midnight and 08:30 NY time), entering continuation setups on lower timeframes (15-minute, 5-minute, 1-minute), partial profit taking, and recognizing the Friday PM session reversal/retracement toward buyside liquidity as smart money covers shorts.

## Concrete Trading Rules

### Rule 1: Trading Sessions Definition
- **RULE:** The morning trading session in New York Index Futures spans from 08:30 to 12:00 (noon) NY local time, the lunch hour is from 12:00 to 13:00 (1:00 PM) NY local time, and the afternoon (PM) session runs from 13:00 to market close.

- **NUMBERS:** Morning session: 08:30 to 12:00; Lunch hour: 12:00 to 13:00; PM session: after 13:00 (New York local time).

### Rule 2: Daily Swing High Definition
- **RULE:** A three-candle swing high consists of a candle with a higher high flanked by a preceding candle with a lower high and a subsequent candle with a lower high, forming only after the third candle closes.
- **CATEGORY:** reference level
- **NUMBERS:** 3 candles.

### Rule 5: Swing Projection Target Calculation Using Fibonacci
- **RULE:** To project downside expansion price targets beyond a broken swing low (fulcrum point), anchor the Fibonacci tool from the highest candle body (open or close) of the impulse high to the lowest candle body (open or close) of the retracement low, and project downward to the -0.5, -1.0, and -1.5 Fibonacci extension levels.
- **CATEGORY:** take profit
- **NUMBERS:** -0.5, -1.0, -1.5 standard deviation / extension levels; anchor exclusively to candle bodies (wicks/tails ignored).

### Rule 14: Overlapping / Stacked Fair Value Gaps
- **RULE:** When a fair value gap has a smaller secondary fair value gap positioned immediately above it, anticipate that price may trade through the first to reach into the second, but use the first fair value gap as the entry zone.
- **CATEGORY:** entry
- **NUMBERS:** 2 consecutive fair value gaps.
- **SOURCE:** 37:48 - 37:58 (Said and Showed).
- **CLARITY:** Clear.

### Rule 15: Pyramiding Position Sizing Rule
- **RULE:** When scaling into a trend (pyramiding), the initial entry must carry the largest position size, followed by successively smaller position sizes on later entries (e.g., 5 contracts, then 3 contracts, then 2 contracts); never add larger size to an existing smaller position.
- **CATEGORY:** risk/position size
- **NUMBERS:** Example given: 5 contracts first, 3 contracts second, 2 contracts third.

## Conditions that Cancel a Setup / Filters (What NOT to do)

1. **No Entries During Lunch Hour:** Do not initiate new trade entries between 12:00 (noon) and 13:00 (1:00 PM) NY time.
2. **Do Not Overtrade Past Session Limits:** Never exceed 2 trades in the AM session and 2 trades in the PM session (maximum 4 trades per day).
3. **Do Not Invert Pyramiding Sizes:** Never add a larger position size to a smaller initial position (e.g., entering 1 contract first, then adding 2, then adding 4). This destroys equity during adverse moves.
4. **Do Not Hold Positions Over the Weekend:** Do not hold intraday index futures trades over the weekend due to unpredictable gap risk at Sunday's open.
5. **Do Not Use Wicks for Swing Projections:** Do not anchor the Fibonacci extension tool to candle wicks/tails when calculating algorithmic expansion targets; use only candle bodies (open/close).
6. **Do Not Use Depth of Market (DOM):** Do not trade based on DOM order book depth or resting limit volumes due to spoofing.

## Gold (XAUUSD) vs. Forex Pairs vs. Indices Differences

- **Index Futures vs. Forex Execution Quality:** ICT notes that Index Futures (CME ES) operate with uniform pricing across the board, providing much higher execution precision and tighter adherence to order blocks and FVGs without broker-specific spread manipulation. In contrast, Forex brokerages trade in internal liquidity pools with variable, widening spreads that can prematurely trigger stops or distort price delivery.
- **Forex Current Market Condition:** ICT states that Forex markets at the time of recording are quiet, subdued, and less suitable for mentorship demonstration compared to Index Futures.

## Ambiguities and Open Questions

1. **Exact Break-Even Offset:** ICT instructs moving the stop to "break-even" after taking off the first contract at the initial target, but does not state whether this means the exact entry price or entry plus 1–2 ticks to cover commissions and exchange fees.
2. **PM Session Entry Confirmation:** At 37:31 - 38:05, ICT mentions two entry styles for the PM short retracement: entering directly at the FVG versus waiting for a "confirmation entry" (a second market structure shift and smaller FVG). The exact quantitative criteria for when an automated system should choose the direct entry versus the confirmation entry are not formalized.
