## Summary of Episode

This episode demonstrates how the 2022 ICT Mentorship model applies directly to foreign exchange (Forex) pairs, using EUR/USD on April 5, 2022 as an in-depth case study. Michael Huddleston ("ICT") details:
1. Higher-timeframe context from the daily chart: recognizing buy-side liquidity sweeps above relative equal highs, setting a bearish daily bias toward sell-side liquidity pools resting below old daily lows and the `1.0900` "Big Figure".
2. Daily and hourly swing projections using Fibonacci standard deviation extensions based on candle bodies (highest close/open to lowest close/open).
3. The Forex-specific New York Open Killzone window (07:00 to 10:00 AM NY local time).
4. Power of 3 (Accumulation, Manipulation, Distribution) using the New York Midnight (00:00 NY time) opening price and recalibrating with the 08:30 AM NY opening price.
5. Managing high-impact macroeconomic news drivers (e.g., 10:00 AM ISM Services PMI) as volatility injections rather than directional predictors, including how 10:00 AM news extends the active trading window until 11:00–11:30 AM.
6. Execution mechanics on 5-minute and 4-minute charts using Fair Value Gaps (FVG) nested within a Bearish Order Block, setting stop losses above intermediate-term swing highs, and accounting for broker spreads ("fluffing" target exits by 3 to 5 pips).

## Conditions that Cancel a Setup / Filters (What NOT to do)

1. **Do not enter short if price is below the opening price before the manipulation occurs:** A short setup requires price to protract (Judas Swing) above the opening price (Midnight NY or 08:30 AM NY open). Shorting below the open without a prior sweep/manipulation above open is low-probability.
2. **Do not hold or enter a short trade if an intermediate-term swing high is violated:** If price breaks above the high of a swing that has an imbalance beneath it, the bearish narrative is invalidated.
3. **Do not enter new Forex trades after 10:00 AM NY time on regular days:** The Forex NY Open Killzone closes at 10:00 AM NY time; taking new trades after 10:00 AM without a high-impact news catalyst invalidates the setup window.
4. **Do not place take-profit limit orders directly at the exact Big Figure or exact wick low in Forex:** Failing to account for broker spread (failing to "fluff" the exit by 3–5 pips) risks missing fills on limit orders when price reverses just above the target.
5. **Do not use wick extremes when measuring standard deviation swing projections:** Projections measured from wicks rather than body closes/opens produce unreliable extension levels.
6. **Do not treat economic calendar forecasts or actual data releases directionally:** Do not try to interpret whether a number is "bullish" or "bearish"; the news is only a time-based volatility trigger.

## Gold (XAUUSD) vs. Forex Pairs vs. Indices Differences

- **Forex Pairs (e.g., EUR/USD, CAD pairs):**
  - New York Open Killzone is strictly **07:00 AM to 10:00 AM NY time**.
  - Recalibrate the open from 00:00 Midnight to **08:30 AM NY time** if 08:30 is lower (for shorts) or higher (for longs).
  - Subject to specific foreign economic events (e.g., CAD pairs impacted by 10:30 AM NY Crude Oil Inventory numbers, extending trading hours).
  - Take profits must account for broker bid-ask spreads by padding ("fluffing") limit orders by 3 to 5 pips.
  - Pricing revolves around Institutional Price Levels: Big Figures (`00`), Mid-Figures (`50`), and minor levels (`20`, `80`).
- **Index Futures (e.g., E-mini S&P 500 / Nasdaq Futures - ES/NQ):**
  - New York Open trading session operates from **08:30 AM to 11:00 AM NY time** (not 07:00 AM).
  - New trades are generally taken up until **10:40 AM – 10:45 AM NY time**.
  - Spread padding is not required in the same manner as Forex pips due to tight exchange-traded futures order books.
- **Gold (XAUUSD):**
  - None mentioned in this episode.
