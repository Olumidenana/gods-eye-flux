# Episode 19: 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 19

**Source URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IEa1N0rTtbc

## Summary of Episode
In this video, Michael Huddleston combines his YouTube mentorship audience with his private charter mentorship group for a shared market review. He addresses broader trading psychology, discipline, the danger of overleveraging, and the importance of adhering to sound money management. 

Technically, he provides multi-timeframe analysis across the US Dollar Index (DXY), EURUSD, GBPUSD, and E-mini S&P 500 Futures (ES). He explains market narrative via the delivery of price between discount and premium relative to dealing ranges (equilibrium / 50% levels), the function of liquidity pools (buy-side liquidity and sell-side liquidity), the "Purge and Revert" concept, and how the core 2022 YouTube mentorship model (liquidity run, displacement, market structure shift, Fair Value Gap / Order Block entry) functions across various asset classes and timeframes. Finally, he outlines how the 00:00 midnight (New York time) and 08:30 (New York time) opening prices define discount versus premium pricing intraday within the Power of 3 framework.

## Conditions that Cancel a Setup / Filters (What NOT to do)

- **Avoid Trading in Low-Volume / Holiday Conditions:** When markets are in a holiday lull (such as Passover/Easter week), volume dries up and price action becomes sloppy, consolidated, and heavily manipulated. Avoid entering active trades until normal volume and displacement return (09:44 - 10:17, 48:10 - 48:20, 58:45 - 59:00).
- **Do Not Trade Counter-Trend Without Senior Skill:** Developing students must not take counter-trend setups against the higher timeframe order flow (e.g., shorting a bullish DXY or buying EURUSD during long-term dollar strength) because counter-trend moves are erratic and have high failure rates (26:00 - 27:00, 53:00 - 54:00).
- **Avoid Choppy / Unclear Market Structure:** If an asset's price action shows overlapping wicks, no clear displacement, and no clean Fair Value Gaps (as demonstrated on GBPUSD), do not trade it. Close the chart and wait (59:35 - 60:30, 62:00 - 62:35).
- **Do Not Attempt to Pick Absolute Tops and Bottoms:** Do not try to guess the ultimate reversal point of a trend. Focus on trading between established intermediate-term highs and lows (16:50 - 17:48, 18:55 - 19:10).
- **Never Overleverage to Chase Fast Money:** Using unrealistic lot sizes in demo accounts conditions the subconscious to accept toxic gambles. Strictly trade micro-lots / 1 contract to develop consistent execution habits (53:00 - 53:40, 114:20 - 115:30).

## Ambiguities and Open Questions
- **Order Block Body vs. Wick for Mean Threshold:** In some sections, ICT notes that mean threshold is the 50% midpoint of the entire candle (high to low), while in other remarks he focuses primarily on candle bodies. An automated system must clarify whether the 50% calculation includes wicks or strictly uses the candle body open-to-close range.
- **Midnight Open vs. 08:30 Open Precedence:** While ICT clearly states that 00:00 midnight open is the primary baseline and 08:30 open is secondary ("last ditch"), he does not define an exact mathematical rule for days when price trades between the two levels without penetrating both.
