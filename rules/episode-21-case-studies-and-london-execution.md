# Episode 21: 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 21

**Source URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kmVXVJE08eQ

## Summary of Episode
In this episode, Michael J. Huddleston ("ICT") discusses macro intermarket relationships, focusing on how the US Dollar Index (DXY) acts in "Risk-On / Risk-Off" environments and how a strong bullish dollar imposes heavy downward pressure on foreign currencies and US stock index futures (specifically the E-mini S&P 500 futures, ESM2022). He analyzes price action on an extremely bearish day where the standard 2022 mentorship setup (which looks for a Judas Swing rally above the opening price followed by displacement and entry) did not form because the market was too heavy to rally. He explains how Power of Three (Accumulation, Manipulation, Distribution) still functioned on a fractal basis, how to read price when the market cannot rally above the midnight NY or 8:30 AM NY opening prices, and how experienced traders adapt by taking continuation Fair Value Gaps (FVGs) with appropriate risk management.

## Conditions that Cancel a Setup / Filters (What NOT to do)

- **Do NOT try to buy a heavily falling market because indicators say it is "oversold":** When higher-timeframe draw on liquidity is lower and the dollar is rallying, oversold conditions will be ignored and price will continue to plunge (11:13–11:35).
- **Do NOT insist on waiting for a Judas Swing above the opening price if the market is displaying extreme heavy weakness:** In an overwhelmingly bearish market, requiring price to rally above the 00:00 or 08:30 open will cause you to miss the move entirely (13:45–14:05, 17:00–17:35).
- **Do NOT move stop loss to break-even or trail it too aggressively immediately after entry:** Trailing too tightly behind shallow consolidations can trigger premature stop-outs during minor liquidity sweeps within a bearish trend (27:30–27:45).
- **Do NOT trade or hold positions overnight or over the weekend in current market volatility:** Positions should be flat prior to the daily close (23:05–23:25).

## Ambiguities and Open Questions

1. **Continuation FVG Entry Rules without Manipulation (Judas Swing):** The core 2022 Mentorship model requires a run on liquidity/Judas Swing above the open/prior high followed by displacement. When this manipulation leg fails to materialize due to a "heavy market," ICT demonstrates taking continuation FVGs on 1-minute to 5-minute charts. An algorithmic model needs precise objective criteria to identify when a market is classified as "too heavy" to require the manipulation sweep before triggering continuation entries.
2. **Dynamic Stop Loss on Lower Timeframe Continuation:** On smaller continuation FVGs, ICT notes that stops should be placed above the structure that allows the trade to breathe (sometimes above a higher, unfilled FVG). Exact tick/point buffer rules or specific swing point definitions for these continuation stops are discretionary in his commentary.
