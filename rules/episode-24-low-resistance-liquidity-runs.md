# Episode 24: 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 24

**Source URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nv0ey5UyOmY

## Summary of Episode
This episode focuses heavily on trader psychology, personal accountability, and the non-existence of a valid setup without market structure confirmation (displacement). Michael addresses community feedback regarding a pre-market observation posted on YouTube's community tab on May 5, 2022. He explains why an anticipated setup that fails to trigger does not constitute a trading loss or failure of the model. He demonstrates on the E-mini Nasdaq (NQ) and E-mini S&P (ES) why no long setup triggered on that day because price merely traded lower without the required displacement above a key swing high. Finally, he presents diagrammatic rules illustrating the two primary PD array entry triggers for the 2022 Mentorship model: (1) running above an old high / relative equal highs, shifting market structure lower with displacement, and entering on a fair value gap; and (2) running into an old fair value gap, shifting market structure lower with displacement, and entering on a fair value gap inside the displacement leg.

## Conditions that Cancel a Setup / Filters (What NOT to do)

1. **Lack of Displacement:** If price taps into a target PD array (Fair Value Gap, old high, or old low) but does not energetically displace through the short-term swing point, the setup is invalidated. Do not enter.
2. **Impulsive Button Pushing:** Never enter anticipating that displacement will occur; wait for the candle closes and structural shift to physically exist on the chart first.
3. **Trading in Live Markets Prematurely:** Do not trade live funds during the mentorship development stage; premature live trading without rule-adherence causes account blowups.
4. **Expecting FVG to Completely Fill:** When price enters an old Fair Value Gap, do not assume or require it to close the entire gap. If it merely enters the gap, reverses, and displaces past the short-term low, the setup is valid. Conversely, do not buy/sell just because it touches the edge of a gap without reversal structure.
