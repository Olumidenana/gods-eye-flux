## Summary of Episode
This episode provides a live tape reading and execution demonstration on the E-mini S&P 500 futures (ES) 1-minute chart. ICT demonstrates trading against the higher-timeframe daily bias (counter-trend) based on intraday market structure and order flow. Specifically, he shows how price swept sellside liquidity below relative equal lows, traded down into a Fair Value Gap resting at an institutional level (4000.00), and held support at an old 09:30 AM short-term high. ICT executes a long position of 4 contracts, pyramids 2 additional contracts into a bullish order block on a retracement, manages his stop loss progressively below newly formed structural lows, partially takes profit on 4 contracts as buyside liquidity is cleared, and trails the remaining 2 contracts until stopped out with a locked-in profit.

## Concrete Trading Rules

- **RULE:** A counter-trend long setup can be taken against a bearish daily bias if price has moved lower multiple times intraday, taken out yesterday's low without follow-through, cleared sellside liquidity beneath intraday relative equal lows, and entered a Fair Value Gap supported by prior market structure.
  - **CATEGORY:** entry
  - **NUMBERS:** 3 intraday pushes lower; 1-minute timeframe; reference level around the 4000.00 level.
  - **SOURCE:** 00:20–00:30, 04:20–04:35 (SAID & SHOWED).
  - **CLARITY:** Clear.

- **RULE:** When taking a long trade after sellside liquidity is swept into an FVG, place the initial stop loss below the lower boundary of that Fair Value Gap and below the recent market structure low (e.g., prior short-term swing high / consolidation level).
  - **CATEGORY:** stop loss
  - **NUMBERS:** Initial stop placed at 3996.25 (10.25 points below 4006.50 entry).
  - **SOURCE:** 00:25–00:28, 05:00–05:15 (SAID & SHOWED).
  - **CLARITY:** Clear.

- **RULE:** Pyramiding (adding contracts to an existing winning trade) can be executed upon a retracement back into a bullish order block (defined by down-close candle(s) prior to an upward displacement) as long as market structure continues supporting higher prices.
  - **CATEGORY:** entry
  - **NUMBERS:** Retracement to bullish order block; added 2 contracts at 4013.50 (increasing size from 4 to 6 contracts).
  - **SOURCE:** 04:30–04:47 (SAID & SHOWED).
  - **CLARITY:** Clear.

- **RULE:** Immediately after adding size on a retest of an order block, raise the protective stop loss order to sit directly underneath the newly established swing low formed by that order block retest.
  - **CATEGORY:** stop loss
  - **NUMBERS:** Stop loss moved up underneath the swing low at 4000.25 (risk defined to not trade back into that level).
  - **SOURCE:** 04:49–04:57 (SAID & SHOWED).
  - **CLARITY:** Clear.

- **RULE:** Identify two tiers of upside targets: a high-probability internal buyside liquidity pool (such as short-term swing highs / retail bear flag highs) as the primary take-profit objective, and a secondary macro objective (such as high of the day) as a trailing/runner target.
  - **CATEGORY:** take profit
  - **NUMBERS:** Primary buyside pool targeted at ~4036.00–4036.25; secondary high of the day pool noted at 4051.25.
  - **SOURCE:** 00:32–00:48, 02:23–02:40, 05:27–05:32 (SAID & SHOWED).
  - **CLARITY:** Clear.

- **RULE:** Take the bulk of the position off (e.g., partial close 4 out of 6 contracts) via a market order as price expands directly through and clears the primary buyside liquidity pool.
  - **CATEGORY:** trade management
  - **NUMBERS:** Closed 4 of 6 contracts (66.7% of position) at 4035.75 upon breaking above 4036.00.
  - **SOURCE:** 05:30–05:32, 11:57–12:38, 13:50–13:56 (SAID & SHOWED).
  - **CLARITY:** Clear.

- **RULE:** Once the trade begins expanding toward the objective, trail the stop loss to break-even or into profit locking (above the average entry price), then continue trailing behind subsequent swing lows.
  - **CATEGORY:** stop loss
  - **NUMBERS:** Stop moved first to break-even / slight profit (4011.75), then trailed to 4018.75 on runner contracts.
  - **SOURCE:** 11:42–11:51, 13:57–14:02 (SAID & SHOWED).
  - **CLARITY:** Clear.

- **RULE:** A bullish order block level to watch for entry/support is anchored to the opening price and upper wick high of the down-close candle(s) prior to the rally.
  - **CATEGORY:** reference level
  - **NUMBERS:** Opening price of the highest down-close candle in the set.
  - **SOURCE:** 08:41–09:27 (SAID & SHOWED).
  - **CLARITY:** Clear.

## Chart Examples

### Chart Example 1: E-mini S&P 500 Futures (ES) 1-Minute Long
- **Timestamp:** 00:07–14:07
- **Instrument:** E-mini S&P 500 Futures (CME: ESM2022)
- **Timeframe:** 1-minute (1m)
- **Date/Time:** 11 May 2022, ~10:07 AM to 10:25 AM EST
- **Reference Levels identified:**
  - 4000.00 Big figure level / 1-minute Fair Value Gap (around 10:00–10:04 AM)
  - Prior 09:30 AM short-term high support level
  - Sellside liquidity pool beneath equal lows around 10:05–10:10 AM (~4005.00)
  - Bullish order block at ~10:11–10:12 AM (~4013.00)
  - Second bullish order block at ~10:17 AM (~4022.00–4024.00)
  - Buyside liquidity target pool above swing highs at 4036.00–4036.25
  - High of the Day buy stops above 4051.25
- **Liquidity Sweep / Price Action:** Price dropped lower three times intraday, swept the previous day's low with no continuation, then swept sellside liquidity below intraday equal lows into the 4000.00 FVG.
- **Entry point / trigger:**
  - Initial entry: Bought 4 contracts ESM2022 at Market at 4006.50 (10:08 AM).
  - Pyramid addition: Bought 2 contracts ESM2022 at Market at 4013.50 (10:13 AM) into the bullish order block. Average entry: 4008.92 across 6 contracts.
- **Stop Loss placement:**
  - Initial stop: 3996.25.
  - Modified after pyramiding: 4000.25.
  - Modified to lock profit: 4011.75.
  - Trailing stop on 2 runners: 4018.75.
- **Target / Take Profit:**
  - Primary target: Buyside liquidity resting at 4036.00–4036.25.
  - Partial exit: 4 contracts closed at market at 4035.75 (+26.83 points average per contract on the partial).
  - Final exit: Remaining 2 contracts stopped out at 4018.75 (+9.83 points average on the runners).
- **Outcome:** Highly profitable trade (+26.83 pts on 4 contracts, +9.83 pts on 2 contracts, realizing approximately $5,500+ hypothetical gross profit).

## Conditions that Cancel a Setup / Filters (What NOT to do)
- **Do not hold or leave stops wide if price violates the order block:** If price trades back down into and through the order block / swing low after entry, ICT states he is wrong and the trade idea is invalidated (stop loss is kept tight under the order block low).
- **Do not rush the stop loss to break-even prematurely:** ICT emphasizes giving the trade room until market structure confirms higher prices and displacement begins.
- **Do not rely on the most distant liquidity pool for profitability:** Do not require price to reach the high of the day (4051.25) to make the trade profitable; always bank the bulk of the trade at the immediate internal liquidity pool (4036.00).

## Ambiguities and Open Questions
- **Quantitative criteria for pyramiding:** ICT added 2 contracts into an order block, but did not specify an exact mathematical percentage rule for how much additional size to add relative to initial risk or original position size.
- **Exact trigger for partial fill:** ICT executed the 4-contract partial close via a manual market order rather than a pre-set limit order, anticipating speed through the 4036.00 buyside liquidity level. An automated algorithm would need a strict limit order price (e.g., exactly at 4036.00 or 1 tick above).
