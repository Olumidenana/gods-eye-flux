## Summary of Episode

In this episode, Michael J. Huddleston examines how to frame a counter-trend trade idea during the New York afternoon (PM) session using the June 2022 E-mini Nasdaq-100 Futures (`NQ`). 

Even when the higher-timeframe daily trend is strongly bearish, intraday price action frequently offers high-probability long counter-trend opportunities. Michael demonstrates how a daily draw on liquidity (relative equal highs) provides the magnet pulling price higher, even if that higher-timeframe target is never fully reached during the session. He details the New York PM session algorithm mechanics: starting at 13:30 (1:30 PM New York time), the algorithm seeks liquidity—specifically retracing into discount (below the 50% equilibrium of the intraday low-to-high range) to test a Fair Value Gap and Bullish Order Block. Michael walks through his live executions (paper trading account on TradingView), entry in the 2-minute FVG/OB, partial profit taking into premium, and explains the psychological mechanics of journal annotation and building "pseudo-experience."

## Concrete Trading Rules

- **RULE:** The higher timeframe draw on liquidity acts as a directional magnet to frame an expansion move, but price does not need to reach the ultimate draw on liquidity for an intraday trade setup to be valid and profitable.
  - **CATEGORY:** general principle

  - **SOURCE:** 02:05–02:37 (Said)
  - **CLARITY:** Clear

- **RULE:** On an intraday bullish day, the 09:30 New York equity market open frequently creates the manipulation low of the day by sweeping overnight/London session lows before initiating the primary morning rally.
  - **CATEGORY:** session/killzone
  - **NUMBERS:** 09:30 NY local time
  - **SOURCE:** 04:25–04:36, 05:01–05:07, 05:35–05:49 (Said & Showed)
  - **CLARITY:** Clear

- **RULE:** A market swing high/consolidation or intraday retracement commonly sets in during the lunchtime window around 12:00 to 12:10 NY local time.
  - **CATEGORY:** session/killzone
  - **NUMBERS:** 12:10 NY local time
  - **SOURCE:** 06:30–06:40 (Said & Showed)
  - **CLARITY:** Clear

- **RULE:** In the New York afternoon (PM) session starting at 13:30 NY local time, the algorithm activates to seek liquidity; if the day is expected to expand higher, it will seek sell-side liquidity/discount pricing first via a retracement.
  - **CATEGORY:** session/killzone
  - **NUMBERS:** 13:30 NY local time (1:30 PM)
  - **SOURCE:** 06:43–06:55 (Said & Showed)
  - **CLARITY:** Clear

- **RULE:** To frame a long re-entry or continuation in the PM session, the retracement must trade below the 50% equilibrium level (into discount) of the swing low formed at the 09:30 manipulation low to the session swing high.
  - **CATEGORY:** entry
  - **NUMBERS:** 50% (Equilibrium)
  - **SOURCE:** 05:13–05:24, 07:02–07:23 (Said & Showed)
  - **CLARITY:** Clear

- **RULE:** In discount (below 50% equilibrium), entry is triggered inside a Bullish Fair Value Gap and/or Bullish Order Block (consecutive down-close candles prior to upward displacement).
  - **CATEGORY:** entry
  - **NUMBERS:** Below 50%
  - **SOURCE:** 07:20–07:44, 09:09–09:24, 10:35–10:48 (Said & Showed)
  - **CLARITY:** Clear

- **RULE:** A Market Structure Shift (MSS) with energetic displacement higher following the discount PD array touch confirms upside continuation.
  - **CATEGORY:** confirmation
  - **NUMBERS:** Not stated
  - **SOURCE:** 07:42–07:57 (Said & Showed)
  - **CLARITY:** Clear

- **RULE:** Take partial profits (scale out) when price moves into premium above the 50% halfway point of the retracement swing, targeting recent swing highs and buyside liquidity pools.
  - **CATEGORY:** take profit
  - **NUMBERS:** > 50% (halfway high-to-low swing)
  - **SOURCE:** 11:20–11:27, 17:45–17:54 (Said & Showed)
  - **CLARITY:** Clear

- **RULE:** Do not enter or execute live trades on a mobile phone; mobile execution is prone to order type errors (e.g., executing market instead of limit) and poor risk management.
  - **CATEGORY:** trade management
  - **NUMBERS:** Not stated
  - **SOURCE:** 01:34–01:50, 12:09–12:13 (Said)

## Conditions that Cancel a Setup / Filters (What NOT to do)

1. **Do not buy in Premium:** Do not initiate long re-entries or continuations if price is above the 50% equilibrium level of the relevant intraday swing.
2. **Do not assume the draw on liquidity MUST be reached:** Do not hold full positions stubbornly expecting the ultimate higher-timeframe target to hit every session; always scale out into opposing liquidity/premium.
3. **Do not execute trades from a mobile device:** Phone interfaces introduce execution errors (market vs. limit fills) and emotional distractions.
4. **Do not ignore time of day:** Avoid entering prior to the designated algorithmic liquidity injection windows (e.g., waiting for the 13:30 NY PM session shift rather than trying to buy mid-day chop).
