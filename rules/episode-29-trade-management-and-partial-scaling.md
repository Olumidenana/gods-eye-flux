## Summary of Episode
In Episode 29, Michael Huddleston (ICT) reviews the trade executions and market framework from Monday, May 16, 2022, focusing on the E-mini S&P 500 (ES) and E-mini Nasdaq (NQ) futures. 

Key topics covered include:
- Establishing a bullish daily bias based on daily swing lows and buy-side liquidity targets (specifically the relative equal highs on NQ at 12,553.25).
- Calendar and news impact: Anticipating lower volatility and a small-range day ahead of high-impact events (Retail Sales at 8:30 AM NY Time and Fed Chair Powell speaking at 2:00 PM NY Time on Tuesday, May 17, 2022).
- Smart Money Technique (SMT) Divergence between ES and NQ on 1-minute charts to identify index strength/divergence.
- A concrete filter rule for short setups: A valid displacement leg requires a Fair Value Gap (FVG) to form in premium (above the 50% equilibrium level of the displacement leg).
- The concept of "Close Proximity Entries": Entering slightly outside or near an FVG when precision entry is missed or execution is delayed, provided price has not run too far.
- Trade management, pyramiding into a position after secondary market structure shifts, handling drawdown ("heat"), and targeting buy-side liquidity pools.
- An extensive address on paper trading, regulatory compliance, and debunking claims regarding simulated MT4 broker servers.

## Concrete Trading Rules

- **RULE:** On the day preceding a scheduled afternoon speech by the Federal Reserve Chairman (e.g., 2:00 PM NY Time), expect reduced market volatility and a small-range day, and do not trade ahead of the Fed Chair's speech due to high volatility risk.
  - **CATEGORY:** news
  - **NUMBERS:** 2:00 PM NY local time (Fed Chair speech); 8:30 AM NY local time (Retail Sales).
  - **SOURCE:** 01:17 - 01:34, 03:15 - 03:50 (Said).
  - **CLARITY:** Clear.

- **RULE:** For a bearish displacement leg to qualify for a short entry setup, the resulting Fair Value Gap must be located above the 50% equilibrium level of that displacement leg's high-to-low range; if the FVG is below 50%, the short setup is invalidated.
  - **CATEGORY:** confirmation
  - **NUMBERS:** 50% (Equilibrium of the displacement leg).
  - **SOURCE:** 05:13 - 05:25 (Said & Showed).
  - **CLARITY:** Clear.

- **RULE:** When analyzing correlated equity index futures (ES and NQ) for bullish confirmation, look for SMT Divergence where one index makes a lower low while the other index makes a higher low (refuses to break its previous low); the index making the higher low demonstrates relative strength and is the preferred candidate for long trades.
  - **CATEGORY:** confirmation
  - **NUMBERS:** 1-minute timeframe; swing lows compared between ~10:00 AM and ~11:05-11:15 AM NY Time.
  - **SOURCE:** 03:50 - 04:36 (Said & Showed).
  - **CLARITY:** Clear.

- **RULE:** If price retests a Fair Value Gap following a bullish market structure shift and an exact limit entry is missed, a "Close Proximity Entry" is valid as long as price remains within or immediately near the FVG boundary before the market displaces away.
  - **CATEGORY:** entry
  - **NUMBERS:** Not stated (qualitative proximity to the 1-minute FVG).
  - **SOURCE:** 07:02 - 07:44, 08:11 - 08:30 (Said & Showed).
  - **CLARITY:** AMBIGUOUS (The exact numeric threshold defining "too far away" or "close proximity" is discretionary and not defined in ticks/points).

- **RULE:** After an initial long position is established from an FVG retracement, a second position (pyramid/add) may be placed via limit order when price forms another bullish market structure shift (breaking previous short-term swing highs) and retraces into the newly formed Fair Value Gap.
  - **CATEGORY:** trade management
  - **NUMBERS:** 1-minute timeframe; limit order placed in the secondary FVG.
  - **SOURCE:** 08:32 - 08:48, 09:36 - 09:44 (Said & Showed).
  - **CLARITY:** Clear.

- **RULE:** Full position profit targets for intraday index futures trades should be anchored to significant buy-side liquidity pools resting above prior session or morning swing highs.
  - **CATEGORY:** take profit
  - **NUMBERS:** Buy-side liquidity level at 4022.50.
  - **SOURCE:** 09:06 - 09:20, 11:20 - 11:25 (Said & Showed).
  - **CLARITY:** Clear.

## Conditions that Cancel a Setup / Filters (What NOT to do)

- **Do Not Take Shorts Without Premium FVG Displacement:** A displacement leg lower does not qualify for a short entry if the resulting Fair Value Gap forms in discount (below the 50% midpoint of the displacement leg). The FVG must be in premium (above 50% of the displacement leg range).
- **Do Not Trade Counter to Relative Strength (SMT):** When an asset refuses to make lower lows while its correlated asset makes lower lows, do not attempt to short the stronger asset.
- **Avoid Trading Immediately Ahead of Major Fed Speeches:** Trading in the morning/early afternoon prior to a 2:00 PM NY Time Federal Reserve Chairman speech carries high risk of lethargic, erratic price action followed by severe volatility.
