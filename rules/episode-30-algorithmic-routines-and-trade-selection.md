## Summary of Episode
In this episode, Michael J. Huddleston conducts a "bottom-up" review of price action on May 17, 2022, analyzing E-mini Nasdaq futures (NQ) across multiple timeframes (1-minute, 5-minute, 15-minute, 60-minute, and Daily). 

He focuses on the mechanics of the PM (afternoon) session following a Federal Reserve Chairman speech at 2:00 PM EST. He explains how New York lunch hour lows (12:00 PM to 1:00 PM EST) frequently serve as sell-side liquidity pools that are swept to trap retail traders and knock out trailing stops before an afternoon continuation rally. He shows his paper execution (buying 2 contracts below the lunch lows inside a morning imbalance/order block) and explains how the algorithm "spooled" price into buy-side liquidity above 12,553.25 during the 3:00 PM hour. He also emphasizes that not every market drop or rally is a valid setup—if the model criteria (displacement and Fair Value Gap) do not form, no trade should be taken.

## Conditions that Cancel a Setup / Filters (What NOT to do)

- **Do NOT trade a move just because price is dropping or rallying aggressively:** Michael highlights that the initial sharp drop at 2:00 PM EST did not present his model (no market structure shift with a fair value gap on the sell side). Even though it dropped hard, it was manipulation rather than a valid sell setup.
- **Do NOT mistake an intraday rebound as "resistance" to short into:** Michael warns retail traders against treating consolidations/pauses during an algorithmic spooling move as resistance levels to short.
- **Do NOT keep stops tight below recent consolidation during lunch:** Stop losses resting just below the lunch hour consolidation are primed to be targeted and swept before the real afternoon move occurs.

## Ambiguities and Open Questions

1. **Exact Entry Trigger for the Afternoon Reversal:** Michael notes that he missed the initial entry inside the Fair Value Gap at the low and entered manually at 12,417.75 after price bounced into an order block. An automated system requires an explicit rule: whether to place a limit order at the Fair Value Gap high/consequent encroachment or require a 1-minute micro-market structure shift after sweeping the lunch lows.
