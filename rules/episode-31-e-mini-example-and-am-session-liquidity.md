## Summary of Episode
This episode focuses on trade execution and trade management on the E-mini S&P 500 futures (June 2022 contract, ESM2022). Michael J. Huddleston (ICT) explains how to trade when missing the opening bell setup by entering continuation moves targeting higher-timeframe liquidity. Using a prior daily key low (May 16, 2022 low) as the downside liquidity draw, ICT walks through a multi-timeframe top-down breakdown (Daily to 15-minute, 5-minute, and 2-minute charts) demonstrating how a bearish Fair Value Gap (FVG) and the formation of a 3-candle swing high pattern provide a precise short entry trigger, stop-loss location, and partial exit framework.

  - **CATEGORY:** reference level
  - **NUMBERS:** Daily chart timeframe; level: May 16, 2022 daily low (3976.50).

- **RULE:** For a bearish entry off a Fair Value Gap, price must trade up into the FVG and print a 3-candle swing high pattern; enter short immediately upon the close of the third candle that confirms the swing high.
  - **CATEGORY:** confirmation
  - **NUMBERS:** 3-candle swing high pattern; 2-minute timeframe.
  - **SOURCE:** [07:02]–[07:18], SAID and SHOWED

- **Timeframe:** Daily down to 15-minute, 5-minute, and 2-minute charts

- **Liquidity Sweep / Price Action:** Price rallied up into the 2-minute bearish FVG, rejected it, and printed a distinct 3-candle swing high pattern at ~10:28–10:30 AM EDT.
- **Entry point / trigger:** Market sell 4 contracts at 4007.75 immediately on the close of the candle completing the 3-candle swing high.
- **Stop Loss placement:** Placed above the high of the swing high (~4011.50–4013.00), aggressively trailed lower above newly formed swing highs as price expanded downward.
- **Target / Take Profit:** Primary objective was sell-side liquidity below the May 16, 2022 low (3976.50). Exits executed in partials:
  - First partial: Buy to cover 1 contract at 3976.75 (limit order at the liquidity pool).
  - Subsequent partials shown on executions: Buy 1 at 3964.75, Buy 1 at 3953.50, and Buy 1 at 3949.75.
- **Outcome:** Successful trade running directly through the daily low down to the session low near 3910.00.

## Conditions that Cancel a Setup / Filters (What NOT to do)
- **Do not blindly enter at the opening bell:** If no valid setup or entry forms during the market open, do not force a trade. Wait for a subsequent continuation setup via a Fair Value Gap ([04:55]–[05:09]).
- **Do not enter a Fair Value Gap without a price action confirmation trigger:** Do not just place a blind limit order inside an FVG; wait for price to enter the FVG and form a confirming 3-candle swing high pattern before entering ([07:02]–[07:18]).
- **Do not leave static wide stop-losses in high-volatility conditions:** Allowing price to retrace all the way to the original stop-loss during rapid intraday movement violates sound money management ([07:41]–[07:51]).
