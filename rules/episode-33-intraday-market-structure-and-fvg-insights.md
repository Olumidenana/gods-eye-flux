## Summary of Episode
In Episode 33, Michael Huddleston reviews the price action for Friday, May 20, 2022, on the E-mini S&P 500 Futures contract (ESM2022). He shares how his son executed trades in a live account (AMP Futures via TradingView), growing the account from $9,751.66 to $13,451.66 (+$3,700 gain on the day) by capturing morning short setups and afternoon long setups.

ICT explains the technical and structural logic required before engaging with the market:
1. Identifying high-timeframe liquidity targets (such as the weekly low at 3855.00 and prior daily low of May 12, 2022).
2. Refusing to trade during early-morning consolidation until a key structural swing low (3915.25) is definitively broken.
3. Requiring displacement and a Fair Value Gap (FVG) at or immediately following the break of structural liquidity, rather than relying on retail "break and retest" or "support turned resistance."
4. Understanding orderflow momentum: when an algorithm is in a rush to reach a macro target, retracements into FVGs may only reach shallow/short-term dealing range premium rather than deep structural premium.
5. Identifying the PM session cycle: observing the market beginning at 1:30 PM EDT, noting the session low, and executing long into the last hour of trading (after 3:00 PM EDT) targeting a premium imbalance / FVG into the market close.

## Conditions that Cancel a Setup / Filters (What NOT to do)

1. **Do Not Trade Chop in Relative Equal Highs/Lows:** If price is consolidating between relatively equal highs and a swing low, do not attempt to sell short-term low breaks inside the range. Wait for the dominant swing level (e.g., 3915.25) to be broken with displacement.
2. **Do Not Trade Support-Turned-Resistance Blindly:** A broken prior low is not an entry by itself. If there is no Fair Value Gap created during the displacement through that level, do not trade the retest of that level.
3. **Do Not Insist on Deep Range Premium During Fast Spooling:** When the higher-timeframe algorithm is aggressively repricing to clear an external liquidity target (like a daily or weekly low), price will often not retrace back to a 50%+ discount/premium of the large macro range. Insisting on a deep retracement will result in missing high-probability continuations.
