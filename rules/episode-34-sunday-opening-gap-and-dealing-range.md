## Summary of Episode
In this review episode, ICT analyzes the price delivery of the E-mini S&P 500 Futures (ES) June 2022 contract following a morning live session. He focuses on two core structural concepts:
1. **The Sunday Opening Gap as a Dynamic Level**: Utilizing the opening price gap created between Friday's close and Sunday's 6:00 PM open as a persistent dynamic support and resistance benchmark across the entire trading week, particularly during consolidative and news-heavy weeks (e.g., FOMC).
2. **Correct Dealing Range Measurement for Equilibrium/Discount**: Clarifying how to properly define dealing ranges with the Fibonacci retracement tool. When seeking long entries, the dealing range must be anchored from the swing low to the swing high of an impulsive expansion leg moving in the direction of the trade, looking for retracements below 50% (discount) into fair value gaps or order blocks.

## Conditions that Cancel a Setup / Filters (What NOT to do)

- **Do NOT Anchor Fibonacci Downwards for Long Trades**: When looking for long entries, do not draw the Fibonacci retracement from a high to a low on a bearish swing to locate a discount level; you must anchor from low to high on an impulsive expansion move in the intended direction of trade (15:38–16:08).
- **Avoid Over-Trading "Sloppy" Morning Sessions**: When price action in the morning does not cleanly form standard delivery mechanics (extended consolidations, overlapping wicks), treat it as lower probability and wait for the clearer afternoon session (07:30–08:15).
- **Do Not Rely on Signal Services or Hand-Holding**: ICT explicitly warns that traders must not seek trade signal rooms or rely on external trade calls, as it creates dependency and inhibits independent chart analysis (04:14–04:44, 12:30–13:07).

## Ambiguities and Open Questions

1. **Exact Sunday Gap Fill Invalidation**: He treats the Sunday gap boundaries as support and resistance, but does not provide a strict mathematical threshold for how far price can penetrate beyond the gap boundaries before the level is considered invalid rather than just a deep test.
2. **Stop Loss Quantification**: In the demonstrated 5-minute entries (touching the 3926.75 FVG and the Sunday gap support re-tests), ICT points out the precise entry tick and target without specifying the exact stop-loss buffer (e.g., whether placed 1 tick, 2 ticks, or 1 handle beyond the candle body or wick extreme).
