## Summary of Episode
In Episode 35, Michael Huddleston reviews the market action on the E-mini S&P 500 (ES) and E-mini Nasdaq (NQ) futures ahead of Memorial Day weekend (May 26, 2022). He explains the key levels of a bearish order block (the candle's low, open, and mean threshold/50% level), emphasizing that reaching into and crossing above the mean threshold signals bullish continuation toward buy-side liquidity above prior highs. He also details the mechanics of SMT (Smart Money Technique) Divergence between ES and NQ around key times (8:30 AM, 9:30 AM, and news releases), showing how to configure TradingView comparison charts to compare lows rather than close prices. Finally, he outlines expectations for days where an early run occurs prior to 7:00 AM NY time (often producing a deep retracement and an SMT setup around 9:30 AM) and cautions against trading holiday periods and Friday holiday afternoons.

## Concrete Trading Rules

### Rule 1: Key Reference Levels of an Order Block
- **RULE:** When analyzing an order block candle (e.g., an up-close candle forming a bearish order block), the three key sensitive reference levels are the candle's low, open, and mean threshold (the exact 50% midpoint of the total candle range).
- **CATEGORY:** reference level
- **NUMBERS:** 50% (mean threshold / midpoint of the candle range).
- **SOURCE:** 00:37–01:12 (SAID and SHOWED on chart).
- **CLARITY:** Clear.

### Rule 2: Bullish Continuation via Mean Threshold
- **RULE:** If price trades into a daily bearish order block and exceeds its mean threshold (50% midpoint), it signals bullish strength and a high probability of continuation to take out the short-term high above that order block.
- **CATEGORY:** confirmation
- **NUMBERS:** 50% (midpoint of the candle range).
- **SOURCE:** 00:54–01:08 (SAID).
- **CLARITY:** Clear.

### Rule 3: Early Pre-7:00 AM Expansion Leads to Deep Retracement
- **RULE:** If price delivers a strong expansion run early in the morning prior to or around 7:00 AM NY time, expect a deep discount retracement into a fair value gap / order block rather than an immediate continuation.
- **CATEGORY:** general principle
- **NUMBERS:** 7:00 AM (New York time).
- **SOURCE:** 13:40–13:55, 14:06–14:14 (SAID and SHOWED on chart).
- **CLARITY:** Clear.

### Rule 4: SMT Divergence Setup Timing
- **RULE:** SMT Divergence between correlated index futures (ES and NQ) typically forms around specific macro/session events: 8:30 AM NY time, 9:30 AM NY time (equity open), or during scheduled high-impact news releases.
- **CATEGORY:** session/killzone
- **NUMBERS:** 8:30 AM, 9:30 AM (New York time).
- **SOURCE:** 07:27–07:38, 14:01–14:13 (SAID).
- **CLARITY:** Clear.

### Rule 5: Correct Chart Setting for Intermarket SMT Low Comparison
- **RULE:** When comparing lows between two correlated assets (e.g., ES and NQ) using an overlaid comparison line chart, the comparison line must be plotted on the "Low" price setting rather than the default "Close" price.
- **CATEGORY:** general principle
- **NUMBERS:** Not stated.
- **SOURCE:** 06:48–07:05 (SAID and SHOWED on TradingView).
- **CLARITY:** Clear.

### Rule 6: SMT Bullish Divergence Identification
- **RULE:** A bullish SMT Divergence occurs when one correlated asset (e.g., NQ) makes a lower low below a previous key swing low (sweeping sell-side liquidity / stop-out), while the other correlated asset (e.g., ES) forms a higher low within a discount FVG/order block (smart money accumulation), indicating institutional buying and imminent upward expansion.
- **CATEGORY:** confirmation
- **NUMBERS:** Not stated.
- **SOURCE:** 07:06–07:25, 08:14–08:34 (SAID and SHOWED on chart).
- **CLARITY:** Clear.

### Rule 7: Pre-Holiday and Holiday Trading Restrictions
- **RULE:** Do not trade on holiday Mondays, do not trade Friday afternoons preceding a 3-day holiday weekend, and limit pre-holiday Friday trading strictly to study/morning session only, as volume drops and institutional order flow diminishes.
- **CATEGORY:** session/killzone
- **NUMBERS:** Morning session only; avoid afternoon session; no trading on bank holiday Monday.
- **SOURCE:** 15:26–16:15 (SAID).
- **CLARITY:** Clear.

## Conditions that Cancel a Setup / Filters (What NOT to do)

1. **Do Not Short into High-Resistance Support:** Do not attempt to sell short when price is retracing into multiple stacked discount PD arrays (down-close candles acting as bullish order blocks, fair value gaps) during a higher timeframe bullish bias (11:06–12:35).
2. **Do Not Expect Typical 9:30 Continuation After a Pre-7:00 AM Run:** If the market runs heavily prior to 7:00 AM NY time, do not expect an immediate continuation at 9:30 AM without a deep retracement and SMT divergence first forming (13:40–14:05).
3. **Do Not Trade Holiday Mondays or Holiday Friday Afternoons:** Trading on bank holiday Mondays or the afternoon of a Friday preceding a bank holiday weekend must be avoided due to low volume and unreliable algorithmic delivery (15:26–16:15).
4. **Avoid Trading SMT Divergence Without Clear Directional Bias:** Do not trade SMT divergence blindly in isolation without knowing the higher timeframe narrative and what liquidity pool price is reaching for, as false divergences appear frequently in consolidating markets (07:40–07:56).

## Ambiguities and Open Questions
- **Quantitative Definition of "Early Run":** ICT states that a "nice early run... before 7:00 in the morning" prompts a deep retracement and an SMT setup around 9:30 AM, but does not provide an exact minimum point or ATR threshold defining what constitutes a qualifying "early run."
- **SMT Divergence Lookback Period:** When identifying SMT divergence between 8:30 AM and 9:30 AM swing lows, the rule relies on visual identification of swing lows without a fixed candle lookback window specified.
