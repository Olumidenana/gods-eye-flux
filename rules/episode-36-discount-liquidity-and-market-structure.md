# Episode 36: 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 36

**Source URL:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pWnMpdN_g98](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pWnMpdN_g98)

## Summary of Episode
This episode focuses on multi-timeframe analysis (1-hour down to 1-minute) on the E-mini S&P 500 (ES) to identify internal range liquidity and the "draw on liquidity." Michael demonstrates how the algorithm seeks discount arrays (Fair Value Gaps) after sweeping sell-side liquidity. He highlights two specific trades from May 31, 2022, emphasizing the importance of Market Structure Shifts (MSS) following a liquidity sweep and the use of the 9:30 AM New York open to trap "retail" traders before the real move occurs. He also discusses his personal rule for Non-Farm Payroll (NFP) weeks: completing all trading by Wednesday.

## Concrete Trading Rules

*   **RULE:** Define the current trading range using the most recent significant Swing High to Swing Low; levels above the 50% equilibrium are Premium, and levels below are Discount.
    *   **CATEGORY:** Reference Level
    *   **NUMBERS:** 50% (Equilibrium).
    *   **SOURCE:** 01:00 (Showed)
    *   **CLARITY:** Clear.

*   **RULE:** During an uptrend, look for buy entries when the price is in a Discount (below equilibrium) and has reached a Discount array like a Fair Value Gap.
    *   **CATEGORY:** Entry
    *   **NUMBERS:** Below 50% retracement.
    *   **SOURCE:** 01:10 (Said/Showed)
    *   **CLARITY:** Clear.

*   **RULE:** At the 9:30 AM New York open, anticipate a quick run below relative equal lows (sell-side liquidity) to knock out long traders before the market moves higher.
    *   **CATEGORY:** Session/Killzone
    *   **NUMBERS:** 09:30 (New York Local Time).
    *   **SOURCE:** 02:20 (Said/Showed)
    *   **CLARITY:** Clear.

*   **RULE:** A Market Structure Shift (MSS) is validated on a low-timeframe chart when price breaks a short-term Swing High after a sell-side liquidity sweep.
    *   **CATEGORY:** Confirmation
    *   **NUMBERS:** Not stated.
    *   **SOURCE:** 02:40 (Showed), 05:30 (Said/Showed)
    *   **CLARITY:** Clear.

*   **RULE:** Enter long positions on a 1-minute chart within a Fair Value Gap (FVG) that forms after a bullish Market Structure Shift.
    *   **CATEGORY:** Entry
    *   **NUMBERS:** 1-minute timeframe.
    *   **SOURCE:** 05:40 (Showed)
    *   **CLARITY:** Clear.

*   **RULE:** Place the protective Stop Loss just below the Swing Low that was formed when taking out the sell-side liquidity.
    *   **CATEGORY:** Stop Loss
    *   **NUMBERS:** Not stated.
    *   **SOURCE:** 04:17 (Said/Showed)
    *   **CLARITY:** Clear.

*   **RULE:** Identify the "Draw on Liquidity" by looking for old highs (Buy-side Liquidity) or Fair Value Gaps in a Premium zone as the target for long trades.
    *   **CATEGORY:** Take Profit
    *   **NUMBERS:** Not stated.
    *   **SOURCE:** 03:00 (Said/Showed), 03:25 (Showed)
    *   **CLARITY:** Clear.

*   **RULE:** On a week featuring the Non-Farm Payroll (NFP) report, aim to have all trading activities completed by Wednesday.
    *   **CATEGORY:** General Principle / Risk
    *   **NUMBERS:** NFP week; Wednesday.
    *   **SOURCE:** 08:08 (Said)
    *   **CLARITY:** Clear.

## Conditions that Cancel a Setup / Filters (What NOT to do)
*   **NFP Week Constraint:** Do not trade on Thursday or Friday of an NFP week if profit goals for the week are already met by Wednesday (08:08).
*   **Lack of Liquidity Sweep:** If the 8:30 AM news release fails to take out the expected liquidity, wait for the 9:30 AM open to perform the sweep before considering an entry (05:48).

## Ambiguities and Open Questions
*   **"Deep Discount":** Michael mentions the price moved into a "really, really deep premium" (03:30), but doesn't quantify "deep" versus standard premium (above 50%).
*   **Trade Selection:** He identifies two MSS points on the 1m chart (05:25) and asks "which one is the shift?" He then clarifies he is playing the run to the premium, implying that context (the HTF draw) determines which MSS is tradable, which is a subjective analytical skill.
