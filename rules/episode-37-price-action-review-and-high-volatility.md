## Summary of Episode
This episode focuses on the "Power of 3" (Accumulation, Manipulation, Distribution) concept and market behavior during high-volatility news weeks, specifically Non-Farm Payroll (NFP). ICT emphasizes the importance of avoiding trading on NFP Thursday and Friday due to a lack of precision and high risk. He introduces a journaling technique involving "pseudo-experience" to train the subconscious and demonstrates how higher-timeframe levels (Daily/Hourly) provide context for lower-timeframe (1m/5m) entries.

## Conditions that Cancel a Setup / Filters (What NOT to do)
- **NFP Week:** Avoid trading entirely on the Thursday and Friday of NFP week due to erratic price behavior.
- **Post-Wednesday NY Session:** ICT advises students to stop trading after the Wednesday New York session regardless of outcomes earlier in the week to preserve capital and mental discipline.
- **Trading "Noise":** Do not treat 1m or 5m fluctuations as random "noise"; they are only understandable if framed within higher timeframe (Daily/Hourly) levels. If higher timeframe context is missing, the setup is invalid.
