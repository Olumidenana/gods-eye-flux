**Source URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=36184dDAqtM

## Summary of Episode
This episode focuses on managing and changing bias when a predicted target is not reached. ICT demonstrates the concept of "narrative"—understanding what price *should* do and why—versus simply looking for entry patterns. Key topics include navigating the New York Lunch hour (12:00 PM – 1:00 PM), identifying bullish accumulation through SMT divergence between the S&P 500 (ES) and Nasdaq (NQ), and the specific logic for a PM session setup. ICT emphasizes that day trading the daily range involves a combination of the 15-minute and 5-minute charts, where the 15-minute chart acts as the "bellwether" for structure and Fair Value Gaps.

## Conditions that Cancel a Setup / Filters (What NOT to do)
- **Wait for communication:** Do not enter a trade simply because price reaches a level (like 4070); wait for the algorithm to "communicate" a shift through a displacement and Market Structure Shift (12:20).
- **Avoid chasing:** Do not buy after a large energetic price run (displacement) has already occurred; wait for the retracement into discount (14:25).
- **Fast Market Filter:** If price is retracing energetically into lunch, don't expect a consolidation; expect it to trade *through* lunch to reach the FVG/liquidity (22:30).
