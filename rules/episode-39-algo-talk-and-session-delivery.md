## Summary of Episode
This episode focuses on the integration of higher timeframe reference levels (Daily and 1-hour) into lower timeframe execution (5-minute and 1-minute). ICT demonstrates the concept of the "Draw on Liquidity" (DOL) using relatively equal lows and a Daily Fair Value Gap (FVG) as targets. The episode emphasizes the "Time and Price" theory, specifically highlighting the Midnight Opening price, the 8:30 AM news embargo lift, the 9:30 AM equity open, the 12:00 PM - 1:00 PM NY lunch stop-run, and the 1:30 PM afternoon session. ICT also introduces the "Low Hanging Fruit" concept—targeting a consistent 5 points in S&P 500 futures—and walks through a bearish breaker setup in the afternoon session.

## Conditions that Cancel a Setup / Filters (What NOT to do)

- **Do not trade** if there is a lack of displacement after the 9:30 AM equity open (01:28).
- **Do not trade live** in the afternoon session if you have already hit your goal for the day in the morning; instead, demo trade or observe to avoid greed (51:57).
- **Do not hold** for higher timeframe targets exclusively; if price gives you the "Low Hanging Fruit" (5 points), pay the trader (31:38).

## Gold (XAUUSD) vs. Forex Pairs vs. Indices Differences

## Ambiguities and Open Questions

- **Displacement Definition:** While ICT shows displacement as long-bodied candles breaking structural lows, he does not provide a specific algorithmic metric (e.g., candle body size relative to average or ATR) to define it.
- **Breaker Entry Precision:** He demonstrably enters the breaker as soon as price returns to the previous up-close candle's range, but does not specify if the entry should be at the high, the open, or the mean threshold (50%) of that candle.
- **Stop Loss Calculation:** He mentions stop losses are "paid" to do their job but doesn't specify if there's a maximum point-based stop loss for the 5-point target model.
