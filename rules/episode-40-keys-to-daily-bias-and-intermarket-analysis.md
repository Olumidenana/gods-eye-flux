## Summary of Episode
This episode focuses on establishing Daily Bias using "Keys to Daily Bias" and a top-down approach. ICT emphasizes that a bias is not needed every day; instead, traders should focus on days with high or medium-impact news drivers. The session demonstrates how to identify weekly expansion targets (liquidity or Fair Value Gaps) and align intraday entries within specific New York killzones. ICT also introduces intermarket analysis between Forex (specifically USDCAD) and Index Futures (ES) to determine "Risk-On" or "Risk-Off" environments to bolster trade conviction.

## Conditions that Cancel a Setup / Filters (What NOT to do)
- **Avoid "Everyday Trading":** Do not force a trade if there is no clear bias or no high/medium impact news on the calendar.
- **Lack of Volatility:** Markets without a news driver are likely to be lackluster; avoid trading them in favor of markets with scheduled drivers (Timestamp: 22:15).
- **Rebalanced Imbalances:** If price has already fully rebalanced a specific Fair Value Gap, it is unlikely to revisit that level immediately; the setup is lower probability (Timestamp: 18:02).
- **Over-leveraging:** Do not use contract sizes that force tight stops purely to fit a risk percentage; use the proper technical stop according to the model (Timestamp: 15:02).

## Gold (XAUUSD) vs. Forex Pairs vs. Indices Differences
- **Killzone Timing:** Killzones for Forex (7-10 AM NY) and Indices (8:30-11 AM NY) have different start/end times.
- **Profit Taking Hour:** Specific emphasis on the 10:00 AM - 11:00 AM window for Indices as the London Close profit-taking hour.
- **News Sync:** Forex bias is often driven by direct currency news (e.g., CAD Retail Sales), whereas Indices follow general "Risk On/Off" sentiment derived from Dollar moves.

## Ambiguities and Open Questions
- **Determining "Likely Weekly Expansion":** The method for choosing whether the weekly chart is reaching for liquidity vs. an FVG remains discretionary and relies on the trader's subjective interpretation of "obviousness."
- **Equilibrium Selection:** The specific highs and lows used to anchor the 50% Fibonacci retracement tool for equilibrium can be subjective when multiple price swings are present.
